Console performance expectations

Digital Foundry's console tests, as reported by WindowsForum, found every version targets 30fps and Rockstar has confirmed no 40 or 60fps mode. PS5 Pro came closest to holding 30fps, with one brief drop to 24fps in a vehicle-destruction test. Base PS5 and Series X dip into the high 20s and sometimes the low 20s in busy chases and shootouts. Series S dips most: it renders at 720p, sits in the mid-20s in combat, and drops to about 20fps or lower in the worst cases.
