Which weapons you can get back

Purchased weapons are tied to Jason and Lucia, so if you lose them, get them from a weapon storage or your vehicle's trunk. Unique weapons found while exploring cannot be modified, and both characters can use them once unlocked. Weapons dropped by enemies can go in your trunk in small numbers, but once lost they cannot be retrieved from storage.
