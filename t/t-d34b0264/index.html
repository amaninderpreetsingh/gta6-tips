Reputation depends on how you play

Your reputation depends on how you play each mission. Finishing a mission stealthily earns Professional, killing too many enemies earns Psycho, and shooting non-lethal spots on enemies earns Hardened. Reputation changes how NPCs behave toward you and can lock you out of some open-world missions.
