Four-gun loadout: ARev, Saiga, MP9, Taurus

One player's pick for the best loadout: the ARev as main weapon with max accuracy mods and a mid-range scope, a Saiga D-12 full-auto shotgun for tight spaces, a suppressed MP9 for stealth and as a backup, and a Deagle or Taurus revolver as a failsafe that punches through enemies. They found snipers less useful once you have a high-accuracy AR. They also say to turn aim assist off for better shooting.
