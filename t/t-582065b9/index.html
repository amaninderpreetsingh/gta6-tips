Adaptive speed limiter on R2

The Adaptive Speed Limiter is a DualSense feature. Press R2 until you feel the trigger resist and hold it there, and the car keeps a reasonable speed for the road you're on. To turn it off, open Options, then Settings, go to the Controller tab and set Adaptive Speed Limiter to Off.
