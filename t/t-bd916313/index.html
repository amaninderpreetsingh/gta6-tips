Disable enemies instead of killing them

Killing enemies makes your criminal profile more aggressive, so go non-lethal. Grab enemies with R2 to knock them out. In a firefight, press L3 and R3 and aim at the yellow body parts, like arms and kneecaps. A yellow X in the reticle means disabled, and a red X means dead.
