Guarded loot spot with Dewey truck and legendary guns

A guarded building just south of North Marina County, above Big Pine, holds a legendary rocket launcher, a legendary sniper, a legendary SMG, a safe and about $30,000 in cash. The Dewey truck inside sells for almost $15,000, is unlocked, has no alarm or tracker and has 6 storage compartments. A suppressed weapon makes clearing the guards much easier.
