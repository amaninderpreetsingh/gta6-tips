Order Lucia's custom gold necklace

The Siren's Chest jewelry store in southern Key Lento, next to the barbershop, sells the custom gold chain for GTA$475. It's the optional errand 'Buy Lucia a Necklace' on the Story map. Pay at the clerk's register and the chain gets shipped, so you don't pick anything up.
