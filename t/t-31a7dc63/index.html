Save and load to respawn warehouse loot

Reloading a save after looting the South Feistdale County building respawns its loot, including the vans' cash and the safe's cash and code. Your cash bags stored in the car stay put, so you can repeat the loop until the stash slots are full, then sell at Brian's lockup. The reel says a loop takes about two minutes.
