OLED HDR black levels at 15

On an OLED, the default HDR Black Levels of 50 won't give true black. Set it to 15 and use HGIG instead of Dynamic Tone Mapping on the TV. Set Max Brightness to your TV's peak nits and keep Paper White at 50, since lower values cut max nits.
