Easy early money sources

Early on, rob grocery stores by aiming at the cashier so the register opens. Gang hideouts, marked by a yellow map icon, hold drugs and valuables you can steal and sell. Drug value depends on type, and you must carry the drugs to a seller. Deposit your cash at the bank so you don't lose it if you die or get arrested.
