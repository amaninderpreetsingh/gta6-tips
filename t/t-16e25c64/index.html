Use silencers for crimes

Get a silencer as early as you can. Ammu-Nation stores across Leonida sell them from the start. A silenced pistol can stop a police report going out for gunfire in robberies, if you shoot before your allies do. Silencers cut weapon damage, but it's worth it.
