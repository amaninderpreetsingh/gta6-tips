Find Paul Walker's Eclipse early

A 1995 Mitsubishi Eclipse is in a garage on Vespucci Island by Vice City, about 5 miles from Jason's house, and you can take it before finishing any mission. One reel says a snake attacks when you open the boot. It is also said to be very fast, maybe the fastest car in the game. One creator suggests doing it after Chapter 1, because you lose your stuff then anyway.
