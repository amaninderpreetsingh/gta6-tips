Install on internal SSD or expansion card

On Xbox Series X/S the game only runs from the internal SSD or an official storage expansion card. An external USB drive can hold the files, but the whole game must be on the internal SSD or the card to play. The download is reported at 212GB, so free up space first, especially on a 512GB Series S.
