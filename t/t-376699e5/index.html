Where to sell stolen cars

In Chapter 2, Moses buys cars without trackers for about $1,000 or more each. He may refuse cars stolen too close to his garage, and the garage has a cooldown of about two minutes. In Chapter 3, Pay 'n' Spray takes cars with Standard Trackers for $4,000 or more.
