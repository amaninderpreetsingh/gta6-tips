Change how your hat sits

Hold L1 (LB on Xbox) to open the loadout wheel, then press R1 to switch from Weapons to Items. Highlight the clothing slot with the right stick and press the D-pad to change its wear style. The prompt at the bottom right reads WEAR STYLE and shows a count like (2/2).
