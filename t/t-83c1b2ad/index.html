Hidden safe code puzzle near Pawn & Gun

Hide from the police and walk up the staircase in the building east, across the street, of the Pawn & Gun in Crosstown. The door shuts and glowing graffiti shows a 6 digit code on the wall, then it disappears and may not trigger again, so record it. The code opens a safe on the ground floor and earns the "The Truth Is Out There" trophy.
