Hidden bunker gives a free expensive car

A trapped compound in North Leonard County hides a bunker with a pricey car. Save first, since you can die to the mines and drones. The owner makes you come back later for a second, harder round, and you must stay with him in the bunker during the crisis to get the car. The side mission makes Lucia dislike you, but you can win her back. Register the car with Moses so you keep it.
