Turn on Xbox remote play

On your Xbox Series X|S with GTA 6 installed, open Settings, then Devices & connections, then Remote features, and turn on Enable remote features. Set the power mode to Sleep so the console can wake when you start a session. Then sign in to the Xbox app on your phone with the same Microsoft account and pick Remote play on this device.
