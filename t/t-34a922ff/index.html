Restart a mission to reopen robbed stores

Sleeping does not reopen stores like it did in GTA 5. The video claims that going to a mission marker with a cutscene, restarting the mission from the pause menu and quitting once the cutscene replays resets the stores. You can then rob them again. Based on one creator's video, so treat it as unconfirmed.
