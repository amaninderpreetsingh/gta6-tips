High contrast shaders make you glow

High Contrast Shaders is in Settings > Display and is off by default. It colours people, items and interactables in the world in high contrast, and it has a Player Color toggle. The same Display tab has a Motion Blur Strength slider and a Dismemberment On/Off toggle.
