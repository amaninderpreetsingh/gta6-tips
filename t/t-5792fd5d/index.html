Creepy statue at a swamp plane crash

At a random plane crash in the swamps just outside Hamlet there's a humanoid statue tied to a tree. Inspect it, and when you leave Jason coughs, the screen blurs and he collapses in the mud. A cutscene with monster feet creeping toward him plays, then everything goes back to normal.
