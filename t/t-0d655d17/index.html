Vice City jewelry stores bring fast police

Jewelry stores in dense Vice City areas like Ocean Beach and Decor District have armed guards and alarms on display cases and office back doors. First response takes only 15 to 20 seconds and dispatch jumps straight to 3 or 4 stars with heavy SUVs, rifles and air support, so a sloppy robbery is hard to escape.
