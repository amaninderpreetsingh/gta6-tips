Garage door glitch for the Starfish Island Bugatti

At the Starfish Island house, you can glitch through the garage door by jamming your car against it and pulling out your phone while crouched, which makes your character spaz and teleport inside. The Bugatti has no tracker or security, so you can register it at any Pay 'n' Spray. Expect several attempts and realigning the car each time.
