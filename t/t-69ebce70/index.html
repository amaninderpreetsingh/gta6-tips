Gym membership for stamina training

Running or paddleboarding takes a long time to close the stamina ring. Buy a gym membership for a treadmill or stationary bike and keep your cursor in the pink area, and you can usually close the ring in one session. The starting area has no nearby gym. If you go to a gym together, both Jason and Lucia work out whoever you play.
