Need for Speed BMW M3 GTR easter egg

In Peacock Estates, Weistale County, about one to two miles from Jason and Lucia's house, jump the gate and go past two roller doors without opening them. Barge through the door behind them to find the BMW M3 GTR. Driving it needs a clone key, which the reel says comes after chapter two or three.
