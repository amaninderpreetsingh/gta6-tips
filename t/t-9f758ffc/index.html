Bring cops to clear a robbery spot

Criminals at a robbery opportunity run away or trade fire with cops when police show up, so you can loot the place unopposed. One star works best. At La Cascara Motel the cops struggle to climb floors, which gives you time to clear the top floor, and one run there earned the Firebug Dominator.
