Smoking boat cocaine during Kenton treasure runs

While driving between Kenton's shipwreck treasure spots you may hear an explosion and see a smoking boat in the distance. Cocaine floats in the water around it and you can pick it up from the side of the boat. Donnie's trailer pays more for it than Brian's lock up, and the player made about $6,500 or more per run.
