Hold back in races, sprint at the end

Race AI slows down when you ease off the throttle, and rubber banding punishes a big lead. Stay in 2nd or 3rd until about 85% of the race, then go full throttle (and nitro if you have it) on the final straight. Players say it works less well in the mud and off-road races.
