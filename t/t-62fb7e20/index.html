Swap characters to respawn stash bricks

After stowing a bag in the second van, walk about 10 meters away and switch to Lucia with the d-pad, then straight back to Jason. The bricks at the Teddy van respawn, and the van and bags stay put. Sit inside the second van when stowing the bags, or the first bag can despawn.
