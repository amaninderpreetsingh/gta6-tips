Silenced .38 in the derelict spy ship

The Assassin Nipper .38, a unique silenced pistol, sits in a compartment in the middle of the Derelict Spy Vessel wreck. The wreck is on the easternmost island of a small island group, west of the start past Abismo Key near Southwind. You can reach it by helicopter from a helipad.
