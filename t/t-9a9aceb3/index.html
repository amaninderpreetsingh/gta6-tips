Secret portal to old Vice City

Activate the radio tower, then hit all 12 substations without using fast travel, pressing the button shown at each one. After the last one you get a text reading U-235 Reactor 1906. Go to the main power station between 7 PM and 6 AM, enter by the side door and flip the lever on its left to get sucked into old Vice City. Come back out with Tommy Vercetti's shirt and a motorcycle.
