Fix Jason's house before Lucia's release

Repairing Jason's house raises your starting relationship with Lucia, and she reacts to the work when she is released. Once you pick her up from prison, the remaining repairs are gone for that playthrough. The steps get fixed during the story, and the paint color doesn't change her reaction.
