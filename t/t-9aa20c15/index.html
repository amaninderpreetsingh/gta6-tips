Raid Illegal Operations for cash

Illegal Operations are criminal bases you can raid without worrying about the law, and they hold cash or drugs. Sell the loot to Brian, Donnie or Bunk, whichever is available in the early chapters. In Chapter 1, hit the Three Sheets liquor store first for a four-figure payout, then the Kingfisher operations in remote parts of Key Lento. Chapter 2 has at least 10 more across Kelly County and Mariana County, and some pay five figures.
