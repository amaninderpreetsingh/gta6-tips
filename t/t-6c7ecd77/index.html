Help broken-down cars for a drug stash

Random roadside events have people pretending their car is broken down while hiding drugs in it. Offer help, and if they don't tell you to leave, search the trunk for a stash. It's an average duffel bag worth about $1,280 in illegal products. Take it to Brian's Lockup and collect the money from your mailbox the next day. These have no fixed location, so keep driving around.
