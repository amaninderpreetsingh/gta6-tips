Reload checkpoints to redo a mission cleanly

To redo part of a mission without loading an older save, reload the last checkpoint from the pause menu. Open it with Options on PS5 or Menu on Xbox, go to the mission panel on the right, and pick Retry Checkpoint. It puts you back at the start of your current section, with objectives and dialogue restored.
