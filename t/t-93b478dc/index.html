Keep spare outfits in the trunk

Cops remember what you're wearing, so store spare outfits in your car's trunk. Pull over, change and blend back in. You can manage the trunk at any clothing store with a changing room, no need to go home. Name outfits like clubbing or hunting to tell them apart.
