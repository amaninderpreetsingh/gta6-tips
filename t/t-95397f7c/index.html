Use blinkers with the D-pad

Get in a vehicle and tap D-pad Left or Right to turn on the matching blinker. Tap it, don't hold, because holding a D-pad direction opens a vehicle or character menu instead. Turn it on before you turn, change lanes or pull over. Controls are the same on PS5 and Xbox with the default layout.
