Check your TV's HDMI 2.1 support on Xbox

On Xbox Series X/S, go to Settings > General > TV and Display Options > 4K TV Details. Green checkmarks mean your TV and HDMI port can handle the console's best features. Those advanced features need HDMI 2.1, and on some TVs only certain ports have it, so plug the Xbox into the best one.
