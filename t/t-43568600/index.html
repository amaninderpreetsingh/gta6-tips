Map shows when a compound is cleaned out

Gang operations show a yellow icon on the map once you enter them. Hover over the icon to see how many key valuables it holds, usually unique weapon variants, luxury watches and a safe with cash. Grab all of them and the location counts as fully completed. It then grays out for a while before it respawns.
