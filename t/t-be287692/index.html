Hamlet factory loot respawn glitch

An abandoned factory just outside Hamlet holds about $7,000 of loot in a van, a safe and around the building. After you clear it, drive about half a mile away and come back: the loot is restocked but the bodies are gone. Repeat until your car storage is full, then sell at Brian's lock up.
