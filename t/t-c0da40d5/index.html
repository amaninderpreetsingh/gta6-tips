Keep the console online in rest mode

To use Remote Play when you're away from home, set the PS5 to stay online in rest mode. Go to Settings, System, Power Saving, Features Available in Rest Mode. Turn on Stay Connected to the Internet and Enable Turning on PS5 from Network. Remote Play also needs to be turned on under Settings, System, Remote Play.
