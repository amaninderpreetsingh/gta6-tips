Return to the Chapter 1 house for $5,000

Once you reach Chapter 2, go back to your Chapter 1 house and check the stash for $5,000 and some photos. You always get the $5,000, because your actual stash is lost. Police are in the area and hard to shake, so take a stealthy route in.
