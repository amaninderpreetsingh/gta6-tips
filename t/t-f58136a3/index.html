Rob the Keys gas station stores

Close to your house in the Keys there is a petrol station with two shops you can rob with a gun. Avoid killing anyone so your wanted level stays low, which lets you rob more stores. Two more shops down the road pay less, and the clerk at one door often carries a gun, so you may have to shoot her. Put the cash back in your house so an arrest doesn't cost you the money.
