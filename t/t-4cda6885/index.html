Accessibility options for tapping and holding

In the gameplay menu's accessibility options you can multiply how many taps each tap counts for, or replace tapping with holding the button. This helps with things like kicking a cop out of your car, which needs square taps. Hold delay, hold assist and hold-or-press (toggle aim) options work in a similar way.
