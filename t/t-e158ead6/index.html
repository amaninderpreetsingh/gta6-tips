Buy garages for more car slots

Parking a car in a parking space or garage only stores it for a while. To keep it for good, register it at a Pay 'N' Spray, which needs a free Stored Vehicle slot. Sell old cars at the Pay 'N' Spray or buy more garages in the Vice City Real Estate app.
