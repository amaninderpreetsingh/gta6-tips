Get the M306 TMR free at a factory

The M306 TMR rifle is otherwise sold at the gun shop only after gold medals in all shooting range challenges. Instead, sneak into a factory you are not allowed in, take the vehicle that moves the boxes, grab the sniper rifle and leave by the back. Ignore the employees and bring your partner to distract them. Both characters then have the rifle.
