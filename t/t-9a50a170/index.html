Legendary sniper and pistol on Bobcat Key

Both legendary weapons are on Bobcat Key, just left of Mariana County and north of Key Lento. A suppressed sniper (FF-SR) is in a boxed compound near the start of the island, and the Turnt Nipper .38 pistol is at the island's northern tip.
