Loot the vans at the stash house

Once the prologue is done and you've played one or two missions for Brian so the stash house is open, head to the dirt patch just north and east of Hamlet. The vans there have packets in the back that add cash as you pick them up, and one van has a shotgun and an AR rifle. You need two duffel bags; another bag is by the left van. Take the counter cash too, then bring everything to Brian's stash house and drop it in the big bag to bank it.
