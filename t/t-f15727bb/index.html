Sell 12 bags to Tyrone, not Dunk

Tyrone unlocks in Chapter 3 after the Kitty Kitty mission and pays roughly $122,000 for 12 bags. Dunk, usable in Chapter 2, pays only about $40,000 for the same load. Approach Tyrone's building from the right side, since the parking lot can get you attacked.
