Raise look and aim sensitivity

All four look and aim sensitivities default to 7, which feels sluggish. The creator uses 12 for both look sensitivities and 10 for both aim sensitivities as a starting point. Leave look acceleration alone, and experienced shooter players can raise aim acceleration slightly to snap between targets faster.
