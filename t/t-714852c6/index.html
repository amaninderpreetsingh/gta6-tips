How Criminal Profile levels work

Criminal Profile has five levels: Professional, Hardened, Violent, Psycho and Irredeemable. It depends on how you commit crimes more than how many. A crime with no aggression (threats don't count) earns Professional, killing enemies gives Hardened, killing pedestrians gives Violent, and mass slaughter of civilians, cops and animals gives Psycho. Irredeemable is permanent. Jason and Lucia can have different levels when separated, so pick one approach and do it together.
