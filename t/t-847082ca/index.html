Car theft needs tools and can be tracked

One-button car theft is gone. You start with cheap cars and basic locks, and better rides need tools such as a slim jim for one car and a key cloner for another, which you earn as you progress. Nice cars may carry GPS trackers, so you may have to outrun the signal before the car is really yours.
