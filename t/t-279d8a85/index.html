Sell loot to the right fence

Loot falls into narcotics, jewelry and marked bills. Most fences buy all three but pay differently. Yasmani in Little Cuba pays high for jewelry and marked bills, Tyrone pays high for narcotics. The first sale is hand delivered, later fences offer pick-ups for a fee, and some give a bigger cut on certain items over time.
