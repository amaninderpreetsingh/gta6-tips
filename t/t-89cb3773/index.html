Free rocket launcher and SMG in an impound bunker

After the mission "Building a Life" you can store weapons in your car, which lets you grab an unguarded Honors HAT rocket launcher early. It is in a bunker in an RV in an impound block southwest of the Griffin Firing Range, north of the starting area in the Keys. Rocket ammo can't be bought, so save before firing and keep the launcher in your car trunk.
