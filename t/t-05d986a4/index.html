Swap attachments from the weapon wheel

You can take a suppressor off away from a store, using the weapon wheel and weapon customization. Hold L1 (PS5) or LB (Xbox) for the wheel, then hold Cross or A for customization, then press Square or X to remove it. Sights, grips, extended mags and barrels can only be changed at an Ammu-Nation counter. The page doesn't say how to put a suppressor back on from the wheel.
