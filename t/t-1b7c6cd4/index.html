Repair the punching bag for cardio

The Heavy Punching Bag is in the garden shed underneath Jason's house in Key Lento. Interact with it and hang it under the house, then start a Cardio workout. It's one of the quickest early ways to raise Cardio, which raises max Stamina. The guide gives no per-set percentage.
