Phone and fire tower lead to a UFO site

On Monte Kalaga a payphone rings only at certain hours, and the emergency phone next to it points at the Stone Hills fire tower. The tower's lookout logbook mentions a fire at Moreau tower. At Moreau tower a car gets abducted by UFOs and drops a phone with a video on it. Nearby, a bobcat watches a rock with meteorite impact marks, and a meteorite piece toward the lake can be sold to Kenton.
