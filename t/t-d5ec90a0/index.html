Hidden melee moves with square

Distance to your target matters for melee. Sprint at an enemy and hold square in kick range to kick them into the water. As Lucia, hold square and push the left stick diagonally left with the camera behind her for a leg finisher. To break an arm, grab an enemy, hold square and push the stick diagonally right, then hold square again and push diagonally left. Try other square and stick combos.
