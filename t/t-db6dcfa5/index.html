Targeting and aim assist are separate

Targeting (partial, lock on, free aim) controls how your aim moves toward a nearby target. Aim assist strength is its own slider and controls how much the game helps you track a target once you are on it. Partial targeting with aim assist at 4 feels good, or use free aim with aim assist maxed.
