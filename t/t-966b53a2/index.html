Five-safe money route up to 100K

A reel route hits several safes for cash. Start at the building on the left side of the map: turn the power on at the back and use safe code 0072, then the safe by the water with code 0212 for another 20 to 30k. Next, the warehouse on the northwest side of the beach: sneak in from the bottom floor, then use code 1886 on the top floor safe for 10 to 15k. Finally the gang hideout north of Jason's house with jewelry and about 10k, and the north gang warehouse with a tower safe (code 5800, about 20k).
