Teddy van stash house brick run

On the east side of Hamlet, go behind the Teddy van and pick up the bricks, about $400 each. Work through the building for roughly $3,600 in total, load the bag into the second van, and sell it to Brian or another buyer in Chapter 2 for money in your mailbox next day. The first visit also gives a verified card you can sell.
