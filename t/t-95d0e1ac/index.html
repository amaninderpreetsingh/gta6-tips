Use binoculars to scout and tag

Binoculars let you scan an area before you go in, check cars from a distance and tag enemies with the right button on the D-pad. Focus mode on binoculars is unlimited, so you can spot loot, entrances and enemies without draining the bar. Focus while aiming a weapon does drain it.
