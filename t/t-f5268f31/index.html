Respawn warehouse loot by driving away

At the warehouse the reel places south of Icedale County, loot sits in two open vans and the offices. Driving about 10 to 15 seconds away and coming back respawns it, with no save and load needed. You can stow up to 10 bags in a second van parked next to the teddy bear van, then sell them at the nearest drop-off.
