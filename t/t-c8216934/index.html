Expect a combat learning curve

GTA 6 uses very limited aim assist by default, so you have to place shots yourself. Each weapon handles differently, and the gaps between guns are more noticeable than in earlier games. Pistols are lighter and easier to control, while SMGs fire faster and are harder to control. The review does not say how hard it is compared with GTA 5.
