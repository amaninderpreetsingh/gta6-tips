Move Randy's list cars off Y Hotel grounds

Some Randy's Wish List target cars are parked at the Y Hotel, and the game blocks you from stealing them there. Nudge the car out of the hotel's parking zone and the block goes away, so you can break in or use the Key Cloner as normal. Then drive it to Randy's garage to cross it off the list.
