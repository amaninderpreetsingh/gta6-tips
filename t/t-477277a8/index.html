Temporary buffs from eating, sleeping, showering

Eating, sleeping and showering give temporary boosts to health, stamina and focus. Any activity that affects a stat shows a pink heart (health), blue lightning (stamina) or purple eye (focus). The buffs last for your play session or until you die. Safehouses change as you progress, so check each one for new activities.
