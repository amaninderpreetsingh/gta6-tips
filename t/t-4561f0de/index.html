Easier combat and car damage options

Go to Options > Settings > Gameplay and scroll to the bottom. Options 24 and 25 are Accessible Combat and Reduced Vehicle Damage. Accessible Combat means you take less damage in gunfights and enemies take more. Reduced Vehicle Damage makes your car less likely to become hard to control or explode. The game warns that they change the balance of gameplay, but they don't alter the story or disable Trophies.
