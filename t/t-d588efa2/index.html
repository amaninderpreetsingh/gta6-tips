Kingfisher W22 at the fishing shack

The Kingfisher's fishing shack in Wetman Bay, northwest of Key Lento, holds a free Kingfisher W22 silenced rifle. The shack is heavily guarded, so be ready to fight first. Once the enemies are down, go into the large shack on the dock and open the case.
