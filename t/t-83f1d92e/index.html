Register cars you want to keep

Parking a car in a Parking Space or Garage only stores it for a while. To keep it for good, drive into a Pay 'N' Spray and choose Register to swap the plates. The fee depends on the car's value. Registered cars can be delivered through the Scooter Bros app on your phone.
