Focus Mode by clicking both sticks

Click both sticks (L3 + R3) to use Focus. In a fight it highlights hittable enemy parts, red for lethal and yellow for non-lethal, so shooting arms or legs incapacitates without killing. Using it outside combat to scout your surroundings doesn't drain it.
